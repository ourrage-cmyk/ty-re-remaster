Reading: the harness runs at high fps and does not reproduce the user's Ultra flicker; T78 therefore needs a low-fps emulation (sleep per frame in `motion.req`) and a per-setting bisect (GI 2 rays, contact shadows, Scope=all relief, AO wide). Medium formal metric (T47) not recorded.

## 6. Other measurements
- AO v2 near-field smear fixed: sand_graze MAD(off,v2) 26.0 -> 1.52, cost +1.44 ms. Relief: MAD(off,on) 1.4-2.9/255 with Scope=all (mostly invisible); the visible gain comes from the detail layer (`env-detail`, +0.3 ms).
- Fallback tests: AO -> legacy, froxel -> raymarch, light -> shadow patch alone validated in staging; shipped policy is effect OFF.
- Hardware: only RX 7600 XT tested. No NVIDIA, Intel or Proton data.

## 7. Risks
- One GPU tested; High (19.8 ms in the v0.3.3 smoke) and Ultra (about 31) are above 16.7 ms; shipped as preview. High/Ultra flicker open (T78, playtest 4). Water pulsing, shadow bright lines, AO halo, footprint glow open (see `recording\INDEX.md`).
- The v0.3.3 High cost rose by about 2.5 ms (GI mask and blur); not optimised yet.
